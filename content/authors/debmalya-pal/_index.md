I am a Master's student in Computer Science at UC San Diego and a member of the HXI Lab. Broadly, my research lies at the intersection of **trustworthy AI and human-centered AI**. I am interested in building AI systems that can reason more reliably and transparently while preserving human perspectives, values, and agency.

My technical interests include **language model behavior, reliability, and interpretability**. Alongside these, I am interested in **human-AI interaction** and how intelligent systems can support people when decisions involve multiple stakeholders, competing perspectives, or complex societal trade-offs.

At the HXI Lab, I work on [CLARO](/project/claro/), where we explore human-centered AI for collaborative reasoning and decision-making on complex civic and societal problems.

I enjoy working across the boundaries of AI and human-centered computing and am always happy to connect with others interested in these areas.
