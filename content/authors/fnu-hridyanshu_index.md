## About

Fnu Hridyanshu is an undergraduate researcher at UC San Diego and a member of the HXI Lab. His work spans **human-centered AI, human-computer interaction, 3D design, computer graphics, spatial computing, digital twins, multimodal systems, and simulation**.

He is broadly interested in building computational systems that model and interact with both the **physical world and human behavior**. His work ranges from physics-based digital twins and 3D environments to large-scale modeling of human, social, and socio-emotional behavior.

A recurring theme across his work is reducing the gap between a person's intent and the complexity of the computational system needed to realize it.

At HXI, Hridyanshu works on the [CLARO](/project/claro/) project, exploring human-centered interactions with intelligent systems.

## Research Interests

Hridyanshu's research interests include:

- **Human-Centered AI and Human-AI Interaction**
- **Conversational, Voice-First, and Multimodal Interfaces**
- **3D Design, CAD, Computer Graphics, and Spatial Computing**
- **Digital Twins and Physics-Based Simulation**
- **Large-Scale Human Behavior, Social, and Socio-Emotional Simulation**
- **Computer Vision, World Models, and Spatial Intelligence**
- **Extended Reality and Immersive Interfaces**
- **Generative Media and AI-Assisted Creativity**

He is particularly interested in combining **data-driven models with simulation**: building digital twins that reproduce physical systems through physics-based models while also modeling how people behave, interact, coordinate, and respond at scale.

## Active Research

### CLARO

At the HXI Lab, Hridyanshu works on **CLARO**, studying human-centered AI interactions and how intelligent systems can better incorporate human goals, context, and feedback.

### ResonantLoom

Hridyanshu created **ResonantLoom**, an object-centric generative sound-design system that makes AI-generated video audio independently editable at the level of individual entities and sounds.

The project appeared at **ACM CHI 2026** and **ACM SIGGRAPH 2026**, where it received **Third Place in the Undergraduate Student Research Competition**.

## 3D Design, Digital Twins, and Simulation

3D design has been a recurring foundation of Hridyanshu's work across **CAD, computer graphics, virtual environments, spatial interfaces, and physics-based simulation**.

As part of the winning team of the **NASA Lunar Loo Jr. Challenge**, he worked on system layout, CAD, mechanisms, fluid dynamics, safety, packaging, and engineering under strict mass, volume, and power constraints.

NASA engineer Michael A. Interbartolo III specifically highlighted Hridyanshu's CAD and mechanisms expertise and his responsibility for system layout in his recommendation.

[Read the NASA recommendation letter](nasa-recommendation.pdf).

His current work extends this foundation toward **digital twins and computational simulation**, including physics-based models of physical systems and large-scale simulations of human and collective behavior.

## Previous Work

### UmeedVR

**UmeedVR**, Hridyanshu's first product, was an AI and virtual-reality platform for autistic and neurodivergent users. It was subsequently **acquired**, making Hridyanshu one of the youngest people to have an AI product acquired.

Developed before the public release of ChatGPT, UmeedVR combined **immersive 3D environments, conversational interaction, and natural-language processing** for communication and therapy-oriented experiences.

The project shaped his continuing interests in conversational AI, XR, accessibility, and human-centered intelligent systems.
