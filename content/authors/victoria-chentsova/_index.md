Victoria Chentsova is a Ph.D. student in the [SDSU/UCSD Joint Doctoral Program in Clinical Psychology](https://psychology.sdsu.edu/doctoral/clinical/). She is based at the Department of Psychiatry at UCSD through the [HIV Neurobehavioral Research Program](https://hnrp.hivresearch.ucsd.edu/) and the [Center for Medicinal Cannabis Research](https://cmcr.ucsd.edu/) under the supervision of Dr. [Mariana Cherner](https://profiles.ucsd.edu/mariana.cherner) and Dr. [Igor Grant](https://profiles.ucsd.edu/igor.grant). She works with the HXI Lab.

Victoria's research interests span the intersection of Substance Use, HIV, Neuropsychology, Behavioral Health, and Implementation Science. If you are interested in learning more or in collaboration, Victoria is open to communication and can be contacted via [email](mailto:vchentsova@ucsd.edu).
